WE DON'T JUST SELL HOMES.

Who we are

WE INTRODUCE
NEW WAYS OF LIVING.

Élan Estates was founded in 2011 on a simple observation: the finest homes were rarely sold by the finest hands. We built a private house to change that — small enough to know every client by name, connected enough to know every great residence before it is offered.

Today the private office spans five cities and twelve markets. The method has not changed: listen first, advise quietly, and represent the residence as if it were our own.

Philosophy

Three beliefs.

I

Listen first.

The brief precedes the search. We do not show homes until we understand how you live — and how you want to.

II

Quietly better.

The best transactions are the ones nobody hears about. Discretion is not a feature of our service; it is the service.

III

Beyond the close.

The relationship begins at the handover. Design, staffing, schools, art — the private office stays for the decade after the signature.
