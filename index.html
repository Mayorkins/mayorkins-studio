GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

If your question isn't here, bring it to the first conversation.

Ask us directly ↗
What types of businesses do you work with?

Small and growing businesses, founders, ecommerce brands, and teams launching or improving a digital product. The best fit is a project with a clear problem to solve and people willing to work collaboratively.

What services can I hire Mayorkins Studio for?

Website design and development, UI/UX, ecommerce experiences, focused landing pages, and website redesign or support. We can shape a project around one service or a connected end-to-end scope.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can review what's working, where people get stuck, and what the business needs next—then recommend a visual, structural, or technical scope that fits.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Mayorkins Studio works remotely with businesses in Nigeria and internationally. Project communication and meeting times are agreed together at the start.

How does the project process work?

We start by understanding the brief, then agree scope and milestones before moving through discovery, design, build, and refinement. The exact plan depends on the project.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, content, integrations, and what needs to be built. Share your brief and an indicative budget, if you have one, and we'll respond with a scope-appropriate estimate.

Can you provide ongoing support after launch?

Support can be included as a separately scoped arrangement, depending on the platform and what the site needs after launch. We'll make responsibilities and any ongoing costs clear before work begins.
